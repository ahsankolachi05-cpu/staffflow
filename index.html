Data security

How staff and payroll information is handled

Staff records and payroll figures are restricted by role: employees can only see their own information, managers see their departments, and only Admins can view or export payroll.

All access is logged, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and records are retained in line with UK GDPR and HMRC guidance in a production deployment.
